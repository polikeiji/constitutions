# Business plans

Rules for the business plan a technology company writes to raise money from investors: what
it covers, what every figure in it rests on, and how the pitch deck comes out of it.

| Document | Covers |
|---|---|
| [The plan](plan.md) | Who reads it, where it lives, its sections, what a figure rests on, how the deck derives from it |
| [Market](market.md) | The segment the plan sells to, and its size as TAM, SAM and SOM built from the bottom up |
| [Competitors](competitors.md) | Who else the customer could choose, what each charges, and the comparison against them |
| [Team](team.md) | Who is on the team, what each owns, the roles not yet filled, and the headcount plan |
| [Running costs](costs.md) | Five years of people, cloud, AI and other costs, each built from what it scales with |
| [Financials and the ask](financials.md) | Revenue, unit economics, cash, and the raise with what it reaches |

The plan is one folder because its sections are written and checked at different moments —
competitor prices on the week the plan goes out, the headcount plan when the team changes —
and together they run far past what one document holds.
